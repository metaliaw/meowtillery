MEOWTILLERY
===========

Turn-based WWII tank tactics with cards. Your tanks are crewed by cats.

HOW TO START
1. Unzip this file into any folder (right click > "Extract All").
2. Double-click Meowtillery.exe. Nothing to install.
3. In the menu, press "English" (top-right corner). The game remembers it.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": it's because the game is homemade and not
signed by a company. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway". It only happens once.

HOW TO PLAY (Muffy and Lieutenant Michi explain it in "How to play")
- Click one of your tanks to select it; click a blue square to move it.
- Click an enemy to make it your target.
- Click a card to play it (hover over it to see how much damage it will do).
- Shoot from the side or from behind: the front armor is the thickest.
- Check the little sign above each enemy: it tells you what it will do on its turn.
- When you're done, "End turn".

Start on Recruit. When you win easily, try Veteran. And if you dare, Ace.
